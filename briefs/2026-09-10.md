**Money / power**

Oil ripped. Brent settled $107.63 (+$6.42); WTI $102.48 (+$6.43) — both highest closes since mid-May, steepest one-day jumps in nearly two months. Mechanism: biggest US–Iran shipping attack wave of the six-month war (Iran claimed 10 ship hits near Hormuz after the US sank five IRGC-tied tankers), plus Houthis seizing Mocha and fresh Saudi-facility risk.
