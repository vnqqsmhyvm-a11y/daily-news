**Tulsa / Oklahoma**

Tulsa’s proposed city-backed legal help for low-income tenants *and* landlords in eviction cases got pulled off Wednesday’s council agenda at the last minute. District 4’s Laura Bellis is rewriting it to lean harder on mediation before court; it’s expected back **Sep 16**. Important: even if it passes, it’s a *resolution* — intent plus a hunt for public/private money — not an ordinance and not a locked appropriation. Bellis has floated a ballpark **~$500k** multi-year city seed so philanthropy will put skin in. She cites ~**13,000** Tulsa eviction filings a year, only ~**3,500** with tenant counsel. Households at or under **200%** of federal poverty get priority, especially with kids.

Same calendar: city paperwork sets a **Sep 16, 11am** sale for **$65.66M** GO Series **2026A** (streets/bridges from the 2019 vote plus parks/community facilities from 2023). That’s a scheduled bond sale, not tonight’s vote.

Settlement talk has been around ~**$11**/mo average if approved; final ruling still looking like mid-October. Summer bills jumped on fuel + interim rates + heat — rates didn’t “double,” usage and generation mix did the damage. El Reno’s Athlon Bitcoin-mine nuisance hearing is still **Sep 14** (city admits it failed to enforce the 2023 stop-work; gallon figures still split ~3.0–3.8M depending on outlet).
