The Lead

US forces struck three Iranian crude tankers today after the IRGC fired ballistic missiles at a US carrier and destroyer. CENTCOM says no Americans hurt; they permanently disabled M/T Downy near Kharg and M/T Stark 1 near Jask, and completely destroyed the unladen M/T Kylo in the Gulf of Oman — vessels they call part of the IRGC’s shadow funding fleet. Adm. Brad Cooper’s line: shoot at two of ours, we take three of yours. Hegseth piled on that Iran’s tanker fleet is defenseless. Tehran calls it illegal aggression and vows a strong response. Fact: the strike. Reporting: the CENTCOM narrative via CNN (centcom.mil itself blocked). Incentive: maximum-pressure oil choke + Hormuz leverage vs. Iran’s need to break the blockade. Nearly 20 laden Iranian tankers sit stuck at Kharg.

Tulsa / Oklahoma

At Chapman right now (late 4th, ~2:00 left): Tulsa 24, Oklahoma State 10. Cowboys’ opener is going sideways on the road. (ESPN live — not final yet, but Tulsa win probability ~99.9%.)
Tulsa County burn ban is on through Sep 8 — severe drought; no outdoor fires (brush, trash, campfires). Gas/electric grills on non-flammable surfaces still OK.
City council’s tenant/landlord legal-rep resolution is still parked for Sep 16 — intent + funding search, not an appropriation. Bellis floated ~$500k multi-year seed to unlock private match. ~13k Tulsa evictions/year, ~3.5k with tenant counsel.
SQ 848 (constitutional convention question for Apr 6 2027) hasn’t moved past the R-vs-R fight: Stitt’s Aug 25 proclamation vs Drummond’s “Legislature must pass a bill or joint resolution.” Both leave office Jan 11 2027 — before that special — so a court or the next governor can still kill it.
