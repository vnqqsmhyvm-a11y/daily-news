Monday, September 7, 2026

Labor Day was quiet here. City Hall’s closed, no new council vote, and the burn ban’s still the local clock that matters.

Tulsa / Oklahoma
Tulsa County’s outdoor burn ban runs through tomorrow (Sep 8) — no brush, trash, campfires, or fire pits; gas/electric grills on non-flammable surfaces are fine. Severe drought status hasn’t budged. Watch for a commissioner extension; a “through Sep 19” county PDF that floated in search is from 2022, not this year.

Next real council deed is Sep 16: Laura Bellis’s resolution on legal help for low-income tenants and landlords in eviction cases. Intent plus a funding hunt, not an ordinance — she floated ~$500k multi-year city seed, eligibility ≤200% of poverty. Data-center permit pause still runs through Dec 31.

SQ 848 (constitutional convention question, Stitt’s Apr 6 2027 special) is still contested process — AG Drummond’s line remains that the Legislature has to pass a bill or joint resolution. Both men’s terms end Jan 11 2027 before that proposed election date. No new Labor Day filing.

PSO rate case (PUD-2025-000075) still sits at the OCC on interim rates; no final order over the holiday.

National / foreign (faith, order, money, power)
After Saturday’s tanker strikes, Iran’s new SNSC secretary Mohsen Rezaei said Tehran will announce an “exclusion zone” outside Hormuz in the coming days — starting from the US naval blockade line into the Gulf — and that Oman corridor maps are “agreed” and due to be signed. That’s Iranian state-media words until maps and enforcement show up. Same weekend, CENTCOM’s Capt. Tim Hawkins called Iran’s claim that it hit an uncrewed US vessel in the strait a “total lie.” Incentive on both sides: squeeze commercial shipping and the other side’s narrative of who “controls” the chokepoint.

Economics / geopolitics, plain
US markets closed for Labor Day. Asia-session futures still priced the weekend: Brent ~$97.07, WTI ~$92.28 early Mon (week-ago gains still ~+8% / ~+10%). Kpler: ~10 commodity ships/day through Hormuz over the past 10 days — lowest since May. Energy Sec. Chris Wright says transits average >9 mb/d and, with bypass pipelines, “two-thirds or more” of pre-conflict flows — admin claim, not an AIS primary. Quotas still aren’t barrels: OPEC+ already held October at September required levels yesterday.

The pump is the clearer deed. AAA national diesel hit a new all-time high today: $5.9015/gal (was ~$5.85 Fri; ~$3.71 a year ago). Regular gas $4.1505 (vs ~$3.20 a year ago). Farmers and truckers pay first; everyone else pays second. Fed still Sep 15–16 — no new vote on a holiday.

Bitcoin
Holiday blank for ETF creations. Last print still the week through Sep 4: US spot BTC ETFs ~+$987M (TFTC table: Sep MTD +$770M across four sessions). Spot hanging ~$79–80k — monetary signal sideways, no new policy or protocol move today.

Sports
Cards split Coors (lost 7–10 Sat, won 10–8 Sun), open at the Giants today. Braves edged the Phillies 5–4 Sunday — five up in the East. OU opened 51–0 over UTEP Friday. OSU’s Morris era started ugly: Tulsa 24–10 at Chapman Saturday; Cowboys host No. 2 Oregon next week.
