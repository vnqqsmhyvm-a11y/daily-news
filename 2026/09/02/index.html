Wednesday, September 2, 2026·Evening Edition

Tonight’s Brief

Tulsa City Council sat at 5. Votes aren’t posted, so this is the docket, not the result. First reading on $65.66 million of already-authorized GO bonds (Series 2026A), tax levy attached, emergency clause, second reading Sep 16. Same pile: a 10-year Cox cable franchise, T20 Mining Group asking out of the data-center moratorium at 5555 and 5665 S. 129th East Ave., plus $3.06 million into TMUA sewer operations and $1.42 million of water-capital transfers.

The new City of Tulsa Planning Commission held meeting one at 1:15, the city-only board after the joint city-county TMAPC. First docket includes a corridor rezone at 71st and Union. Don’t treat that as approved until they say so.

El Reno is the Oklahoma bitcoin story, and it is ugly. After last night’s brief, the city said it failed to enforce a June 2023 stop-work at Athlon’s Jensen Road mine. Leak estimates split, about 3.0 million gallons in the Journal Record versus about 3.8 million on News9 and KOCO. Property condemned Friday. Nuisance hearing Sep 14. Interim city manager Ken Brown said the city failed. City says the owner pays, not the taxpayers. Gallons aren’t reconciled, and unlawful operation is a city claim, not a court finding. Mining load plus water, and a government that sat on its own order for three years.

Muscogee National Council voted 11-3 Saturday to take tribal employee min wage to $20 by Oct 1, 2028, about $52.5 million over three years. Health, OneFire, and gaming excluded. Statewide floor is still $7.25. Tribal employer, not a state mandate. Journal Record put it out today.

Rogers County passed a 14-day emergency burn ban through Sep 15. House GOP closed Rules hearings start Sep 8 on Rep. Danny Williams of Seminole, staff complaints and possible misuse of state resources. Allegations aren’t public. That’s the majority party opening a file on its own member.

A construction worker died today in Sand Springs near Morrow and 97/412. Not the extraction blast. Outlets disagree on trench versus curb. OSHA is there.

Two Filipino seafarers are dead on Saudi VLCC Sidr after a Hormuz incident Monday night. Bahri confirmed it today. Saudi MFA calls it Iran targeting the tanker. UKMTO confirmed two fatalities from the Aug 31 strait incident. Maritime firms say unknown projectiles hit Sidr and another VLCC, Senegal Prosperity, minutes apart off Oman. IRGC says two tankers hit mines on an illegal US-urged route. CENTCOM, in a Wednesday social post quoted by CNBC: no ships have hit mines, IRGC disinformation. Wright told CNBC more than 17 million barrels moved through Hormuz by ship Monday, a wartime high, versus about 20 million a day before the war. Trump posted he isn’t bargaining, that the US has almost total control of the strait, and Iran’s economy is collapsing. Words. The deed is dead crew and a war-risk oil bid. Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian strikes today. Iran’s health minister put US-strike casualties at 18 killed and 108 hurt, including the Sirik wedding the Pentagon said it is looking into.

Brent settled $95.63. WTI $91.01. EIA week ending Aug 28: commercial crude down 4.5 million barrels to 424.5. Cushing stocks 22.5 million, up a hair. SPR 286.6 million, down 3.1 on the week and down 29% from a year ago. Distillate 14% below the five-year average. Regular gasoline $4.071 as of Aug 31, diesel $5.599. Four-week product demand 20.4 million barrels a day, down 4% year over year. Crude drew, demand is soft, the buffer is thinner, the pump is not cheap. EIA’s weekly Cushing spot is $84.57. That is not today’s futures settle.

Ten-year closed Sep 1 at 4.79%. MBA: purchase apps up 2% last week, 30-year conforming 6.79%, ARM share 8% as people stretch.

Ukraine, short: drones into central Kyiv this morning, at least 13 injured, seventh straight day. Two civilians killed in Dnipro, per AP. Putin at the SCO would not rule out striking UK military sites if UK arms keep coming. Threat, not a strike.

Bitcoin: no protocol or policy move, and the coin didn’t actually move. El Reno is the bitcoin news.

Sports: Cards beat the Dodgers 13-8 last night, again tonight at 9:10 CT. Braves lost 5-9 in Washington, then won 9-0 this afternoon. OU Friday vs UTEP. OSU Saturday vs Murray State.
