Wednesday, September 9, 2026

Tulsa County did not let the burn ban die yesterday — commissioners extended it through Sep 21 after fire chiefs said extreme fire danger is still here. Grills on non-flammable surfaces still OK; open burning is not.

TPS put the hard options on the record at Tuesday’s board: closures/consolidation, salary freezes, bigger classes, cutting counseling/athletics/arts, and ending magnet busing — all on the table to cut at least $12M by the Oct 19 plan deadline. Community input sessions start today. Citywide money problem, not a classroom culture fight.

On the power bill: OCC still has no final order in PSO PUD-2025-000075. ALJ report sat since Aug 21; interim rates have been live since July 1 under the 180-day statute. OCC told local media PSO could put in about +$25/mo interim; the stipulation-with-offsets figure people hear (~+$11) is not a new PSO “offer” — final is whatever the commission orders, with refunds if they cut it.

SQ 848: former Rep. Mark McBride filed at the Oklahoma Supreme Court Tuesday asking the court to block Stitt’s Apr 6, 2027 special election and the companion convention commission — argument is only the Legislature can put that question on the ballot. AG Drummond’s office already called the governor’s move unconstitutional; Stitt says the constitution requires the 20-year vote. Court has not assumed jurisdiction or issued a stay.

National / foreign. CENTCOM says U.S. forces destroyed five IRGC-tied crude carriers Sep 8 after two IRGC ballistic-missile attempts on a U.S. warship — ship evaded, no U.S. casualties, crews ordered off before hits. Iran answered with claimed strikes on ships near Hormuz and missiles toward a Jordan base (Jordan says most intercepted, no casualties reported). Incentive: Washington is running a tanker-for-tanker economic war to choke IRGC oil cash and keep a U.S.-escorted Hormuz lane open; Tehran is trying to own the strait and make transit insurance and fuel costs the West’s problem. Treat IRGC damage claims as claims until third-party confirmation.

Economics / energy. Oil settled hard: WTI $96.05 (+~$3), Brent $101.21 (back over $100). AAA national regular $4.2245; diesel $5.9424 — AAA’s own table marks that diesel print as the highest recorded national average (9/9/26). Oklahoma AAA: regular ~$4.27, diesel ~$5.50. EIA’s weekly petroleum report is not out today — Labor Day delay to Thu Sep 10, ~11am ET. 10-year yield 4.80% (FRED, Sep 8). Freddie 30-year mortgage survey 6.71% (week of Sep 3). Markets still price roughly ~60% odds of a +25 bp hike at the Sep 15–16 FOMC (secondary FedWatch cites) with CPI Friday Sep 11. Higher crude → diesel → freight and food; hike odds keep mortgage credit tight.

Bitcoin. Spot ETF flows: −$46.6M on Sep 8 (Farside/TFTC; GBTC led outflows). September MTD still +$723.5M through Sep 8. Sep 9 Farside row looks incomplete — not treated as final. Price soft near high-$70ks — flow is the signal, not a protocol change.

Sports. Cardinals lost 2–1 at San Francisco Tuesday. Braves got hammered 7–1 by the Rays Tuesday. OU and OSU idle this week — Cowboys host #2 Oregon Saturday 11am CT after the Tulsa loss; Sooners next Sep 12.
