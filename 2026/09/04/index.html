Friday, September 4, 2026

Tulsa / Oklahoma

Council’s tenant-and-landlord legal-aid resolution is still the live local deed: pulled off the Sep 2 agenda so Bellis can add mediation language, back for a vote Sep 16. It’s a resolution of intent plus a funding hunt, not an ordinance and not appropriated cash. Bellis’s ballpark is ~$500k multi-year city seed money to unlock private dollars; eligibility ≤200% of poverty. She cites ~13k Tulsa evictions a year, ~3.5k with tenant counsel. (Flyer)

At the capitol, Stitt’s Aug 25 proclamation trying to put a constitutional-convention question (SQ 848) on an April 6, 2027 special election is still R-vs-R. The Oklahoma Constitution says the question goes to voters at least every 20 years — last time was 1970 — but it doesn’t name who forces it when the Legislature sits on it. AG Drummond says only a bill or joint resolution signed by the governor can refer it. Both men’s terms end Jan 11, 2027, before that April date. Voters have rejected convention questions before. (Ballotpedia)

National / foreign (faith, order, money, power)

Hormuz is still a managed stalemate, not an open strait. CNN’s Sep 4 analysis: US officials claim a Tuesday wartime-high escort of 40 commercial ships / ~18 million barrels; Trump is publicly happy with “almost total control” and no rush to talk. Iran is answering with limited strikes (Jordan, Bahrain, Kuwait this week) and keeping commercial traffic disrupted. What Americans feel: AAA national gas ~$4.14 (vs ~$3.19 a year ago); SPR drawn to its lowest since 1983; unnamed inventory sources say ~80% of pre-war THAAD interceptors and about half the Patriots are gone. Words vs deeds: rhetoric of control, inventories and interceptors doing the paying.

Economics / geopolitics (plain)

The deed today is the jobs print. BLS: August nonfarm +162,000; unemployment stuck at 4.1%; average hourly earnings +3.1% year over year. Food services +59k, local-government education +42k; information −23k. June and July revised up a combined 55k (July flipped from −23k to +21k). That’s hotter than the soft trend Waller was talking about Wednesday.

Markets priced more of a September hike — short-rate futures roughly high-50s / ~60% for +25 bp at the Sep 15–16 FOMC (still a coin-flip family, not a vote). 10-year around 4.78%. Inflation print next week is still the real lever. Funds rate sits 3.50–3.75%.

Oil: WTI ~$91–92, Brent ~$95–96 depending on the tick — war premium intact while Hormuz stays thin.
